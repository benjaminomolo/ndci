National Programmes

Strategic Development Priorities

Three flagship programmes coordinate local organisations to deliver integrated, accountable, and sustainable development across South Sudan.

🎓

Education Sector

National Education Revitalization Program

Building Skills, Schools, and Futures — improving learning outcomes through qualified teachers, rehabilitated schools, and vocational training centers.

  • National Teacher Training Institutes (NTTIs)
  • 500 schools constructed or rehabilitated
  • Vocational and Technical Training Centers (VTTCs)
  • Girls' education and community mobilisation
Target: 15,000 teachers | 100,000 youth trained Estimated Budget: USD 50 Million
🏥

Health Sector

National Health Access and Resilience Program

Strengthening Systems, Saving Lives — expanding access to essential healthcare and building capacity in human resources for health.

  • Training nurses, midwives, and community health workers
  • 250 health facilities rehabilitated or constructed
  • Mobile clinics for hard-to-reach populations
  • Mental health and psychosocial support integration
Target: 8,000 healthcare workers trained Estimated Budget: USD 70 Million
🎭

Culture & Tourism Sector

Cultural Rebirth and Tourism Empowerment Program

Building Unity, Creativity, and Opportunity — using culture and creative arts for peacebuilding, national identity, and economic growth.

  • National and regional Cultural Centers and Museums
  • Youth training in music, theatre, film, and visual arts
  • Inter-state cultural festivals and exhibitions
  • Tourism and hospitality infrastructure development
Target: Youth employment in arts, sports, and tourism Estimated Budget: USD 30 Million

About the Consortium

A Unified Platform for Local Organisations

The National Development Consortium Initiative (NDCI) is an independent coordinating and implementing entity that brings together local NGOs, community-based organisations, academic institutions, faith-based organisations, youth associations, and private sector partners.

The consortium fosters synergy among existing initiatives, reduces duplication of efforts, and enhances both access to funding and the efficient use of resources. By harmonizing diverse interventions and strategically directing resources to the most impactful areas, NDCI seeks to achieve sustainable and measurable development outcomes.

Our Vision

A unified civil society platform driving inclusive national development and social transformation through education, health, and cultural empowerment.

Our Mission

To coordinate and amplify the impact of local NGOs and community organisations, ensuring accountability, synergy, and alignment with national priorities.

Consortium Structure

01

Board of Trustees

Strategic oversight, policy direction, and accountability. Includes development experts, civil society leaders, and private sector representatives.

02

Executive Directorate

Overall coordination, donor relations, and programme leadership headed by an Executive Director.

03

Sectoral Departments

Education & Skills, Health & Wellbeing, Culture, Arts & Tourism, Finance & Administration, MEL Unit, and Partnerships.

04

Regional Implementation Offices

10 State offices and 3 Administrative Area offices with Regional Coordinators and Community Advisory Councils.
